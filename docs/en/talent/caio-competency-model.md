# CAIO Competency Five-Dimension Model

> Core Framework of George Methodology

## 🎯 Model Overview

The CAIO (Chief AI Officer) Competency Five-Dimension Model is a systematic framework for assessing and developing AI executives.

**Five-Dimension Weight Distribution:**

```
    Strategic Thinking (30%)
            /\
           /  \
          /    \
 Talent Acquisition  Organization Design
   (20%)  \   /  (25%)
            \/
            /\
           /  \
 Governance (15%) Change Management (10%)
```

---

## 📊 Five Dimensions Explained

### 1️⃣ Strategic Thinking (30%)

**Core Capabilities:**
- Technology trend insight: Understand AI technology development path
- Business transformation: Convert AI technology into business value
- Competitive landscape analysis: Grasp industry AI competition situation
- Investment decision support: Evaluate AI project ROI

**Assessment Indicators:**
- Can clearly articulate 3-year enterprise AI strategic plan
- Can identify AI technology commercialization opportunities
- Can evaluate AI investment risks and returns

---

### 2️⃣ Organization Design (25%)

**Core Capabilities:**
- Team architecture design: Design efficient AI team structure
- Talent pipeline development: Build AI talent pipeline
- Culture shaping: Establish AI innovation culture
- Cross-department collaboration: Break down data silos

---

### 3️⃣ Talent Acquisition (20%)

**Core Capabilities:**
- Executive network: Have top AI talent connections
- Assessment system: Establish scientific talent evaluation methods
- Employer branding: Build AI talent-attractive employer image
- Search channels: Diversified talent acquisition channels

---

### 4️⃣ Governance & Compliance (15%)

**Core Capabilities:**
- AI ethics: Understand and apply AI ethical principles
- Data security: Master data protection regulations
- Algorithm transparency: Ensure AI decision explainability
- Compliance management: Establish AI compliance system

---

### 5️⃣ Change Management (10%)

**Core Capabilities:**
- Change driving: Drive organizational AI transformation
- Interest coordination: Balance various stakeholder interests
- Communication influence: Influence executive decision-making
- Resistance resolution: Handle change resistance
