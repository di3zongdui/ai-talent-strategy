# George AI Talent Pyramid

> Strategic Layer Methodology

## 📊 Model Overview

The AI Talent Pyramid is a four-layer architecture model for guiding enterprises to build a complete AI talent system.

```
          ┌─────────────────────────┐
          │    AI Strategy Council   │  ← Board Level (3-5 people)
          │    (Salary: $750K+)      │
          ├─────────────────────────┤
          │       CAIO/CDO          │  ← C-Level (1 person)
          │   (Salary: $300K-$750K)  │
          ├─────────────────────────┤
          │   AI Product/Tech VP    │  ← VP Level (2-5 people)
          │   (Salary: $120K-$300K)  │
          ├─────────────────────────┤
          │  AI Engineers/Experts   │  ← Execution Level (10-100 people)
          │    (Salary: $45K-$120K)  │
          └─────────────────────────┘
```

## 🎯 Layer Details

### Layer 1: AI Strategy Council (Board Level)

**Responsibilities:**
- AI strategic direction decisions
- Major AI investment approvals
- AI ethics and compliance oversight

**Composition:**
- CEO/Chairman (Chair)
- CTO/VP of Technology
- External AI advisors (1-2 people)
- Independent director representative

**Meeting Frequency:** Quarterly

---

### Layer 2: CAIO/CDO (C-Level)

**Full Title:** Chief AI Officer / Chief Data Officer

**Core Responsibilities:**
1. Develop enterprise AI strategic roadmap
2. Coordinate AI project resource allocation
3. Establish AI governance framework
4. Report AI progress to the board

**Competency Requirements:**
- Technical background + business acumen
- Cross-department coordination ability
- Executive communication and influence

---

### Layer 3: AI Product/Tech VP (VP Level)

**Role Types:**
- VP of AI Products
- VP of AI Technology/CTO
- VP of Data Science
- VP of AI Engineering

**Team Size:** 10-50 people

---

### Layer 4: AI Engineers/Experts (Execution Level)

**Role Categories:**
| Category | Example Roles | Skills Required |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Algorithm | ML Engineer | Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch |
| Engineering | MLOps Engineer | K8s, Docker, CI/CD |
| Data | Data Engineer | SQL, Spark, ETL |
| Product | AI Product Manager | Requirements, Prototyping |

---

## 💡 Application Recommendations

### Startup (10-50 people)
- Recommended: CAIO + 3-5 AI engineers
- Priority: Full-stack AI engineers

### Growth Company (50-200 people)
- Recommended: CAIO + AI Tech VP + 10-20 AI team
- Establish dedicated AI department

### Large Enterprise (2000+ people)
- Recommended: Complete pyramid architecture
- Establish AI Strategy Council
- Build AI Center of Excellence
